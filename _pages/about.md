Vishnu Ajith is still trying to figure out who he is. So an 'about me' is certainly a daunting task. Professionally, he is a computers guy. Currently working as an AI Engineer, he used to write compilers, code quantum computers, design GPUs and help build FOSS communities at various parts of his career. 

An introvert who loves his books and music, he is also known to travel often and loves trying new food. He is trying a hand at writing whenever the occasional whim strikes. An apostate to strict schedules, this third culture kid is now adulting as an expatriate in Ireland.
