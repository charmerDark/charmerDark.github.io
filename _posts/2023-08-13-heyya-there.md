Cheers to finding my substack! Hi I am Vishnu and this is an attempt at documenting some parts of my life. I am still not very certain what I will post here, but I reckon it will be a mix and mash of photography, shower thoughts, musings, book reviews and maybe even an odd instructional or technical article.

I wanted a space of my own outside major social media. The initial idea was to host a little retro website of mine, but I also madly want feedback and hosting a website which allows for feedback costs money. Maybe I will make a series about the website I build for myself eventually. For now, to use the cliched startup reference, "Move fast, break things". I will try out this platform and if it sticks, it sticks.

I have been experiencing change in most spheres of my life recently. I've just shifted cities, started my first in office job (thanks covid) and am doing a significant part of "adulting" for the first time. This will be a space for me to reflect and for you the reader, to see some of what I have been up to, pick up some useful tips and maybe avoid some of the mistakes I make.

I also like to dabble in new things and though I don't get too good at most of them, I have made some amazing memories on the way. This might be a space to showcase some of it. My amateur photography off phone cameras, (The trick is to go to places so pretty even the camera and your skills can't hamper the photo), my reading about different ideologies and lifestyles, board games, software, the free and open source movement, might even narrate an audiobook and post it here, a story or poem of my own might make an appearance. I plan to try a hand at sketching, so might update the progress. But then none of this might happen too. We'll see. Welcome to my substack once again!

PS: I am mad about feedback, so please please feel free to drop me a line in the comments or on the chat option in the site. That might be what keeps this substack thing going even.
